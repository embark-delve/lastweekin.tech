WIRED General Tech

NASA’s Artemis II mission, after a 10‑day lunar excursion, is set to re‑enter Earth’s atmosphere on Friday, April 10 at 5:07 p.m. PDT (with corresponding U.S. times from 5:07 p.m. SF to 8:08 p.m. NY). Shock from Iran war has Trump's vision for US energy dominance flailing

Ars Technica AI

Trump’s claim that the U.S. can weather the chaos sparked by Iran’s blockade of the Strait of Hormuz ignores the sharp reality seen at pumps across America, where gasoline prices have spiked by more than $4 per gallon—an $8.4 billion hike in a month—illustrating the nation’s vulnerability to global supply shocks. While the U.S. is the world’s largest oil and gas producer, it also consumes the most, making it heavily dependent on the same international market that the conflict has disrupted. Iran’s insistence on controlling the Strait—and its threat to charge tolls—has kept tankers stalled, and even a two‑week ceasefire has not fully restored flow. The Energy Information Administration warns that elevated prices will persist through year‑end, and critical infrastructure such as Qatar’s Ras Laffan LNG terminal may suffer long‑term damage. As the U.S. rolls back climate and clean‑energy policies, its dual role as producer and consumer leaves it exposed to the very global disruptions it claims to dominate.

Why Is It So Hard to Fix an Electric Bike? (2026)

WIRED General Tech

Bike shops are increasingly wary of taking on electric‑bike repairs because the market is littered with vehicles that don’t meet the legal Class 1‑3 definition—many sold online exceed the 28 mph limit and function more like mopeds than bicycles. Owners such as MacKenzie Hardt of Hardt Family Cyclery now refuse to repair any ebike without UL 2849 certification, citing safety and liability concerns after witnessing injuries from poorly controlled power systems. With nationwide injury rates for e‑bikes up by over 1,000 % since 2020, insurers often void coverage on non‑compliant bikes, leaving shops exposed to product‑liability claims and legal uncertainty that varies state to state.
