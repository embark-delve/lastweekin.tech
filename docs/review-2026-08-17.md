## 1. The site is not served

Verified 2026-08-17:

```
dig +short A lastweekin.tech        -> (empty)
dig +short A www.lastweekin.tech    -> (empty)
dig +short NS lastweekin.tech       -> ns-cloud-d{1,4}.googledomains.com
curl https://lastweekin.tech        -> 000 (no connection)
```

The domain has Google Cloud DNS nameservers and **no A or AAAA record on either
the apex or `www`**. `vercel.json` pins `outputDirectory: public`, and
`public/index.html` renders correctly — but nothing is reachable.

The consequence is worth stating plainly: **44 archived editions, published
weekly and unbroken since 2025-10-25, have had zero readers** — not because of
the content, but because there is no address. `CLAUDE.md` notes the repo is
private and that the host serves `public/` verbatim; it does not record that no
host is currently attached.

This is the single highest-value action available, and it needs a DNS record
rather than any code.

## 2. The publish gate is not enforcing its own threshold

**Verified against the currently rendered edition**, `data/latest.json` and
`public/index.html`:

| #   | Title                                          | Summary                                     | `has_summary()`                            |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | New official 30th anniversary Quake mission p… | **26 chars** ("Bethesda marked Quake's 30") | **False** — below `MIN_SUMMARY_CHARS = 40` |
| 4   | London still dominates Britain's datacenter m… | **empty**                                   | **False**                                  |

Two stories fail `has_summary`, against `max_missing_summaries = 1`
(`config.py:54`). `validate_digest` should therefore have returned a problem and
`assert_publishable` should have raised `DigestValidationError`. The edition
published anyway, and **both stories are rendered on the live page** — so this
is a publish-time failure, not an artifact of the git-history backfill.

Worth distinguishing before fixing: whether `assert_publishable` is actually
called on the scheduled path, whether a config override raises the limit at
runtime, or whether summaries are populated after the gate runs. The three are
easy to tell apart and lead to different fixes.

Story 1 is the more interesting case — a 26-character fragment reads as a real
summary to a human skim and is invisible unless the length rule is applied.

## 3. Empty summaries: improving, but plateaued

Measured across all 44 archived editions, 308 summaries:

| Cohort     | Editions | Summaries | Empty           |
| ---------- | -------- | --------- | --------------- |
| Older half | 22       | 154       | 104 (**67.5%**) |
| Newer half | 22       | 154       | 36 (**23.4%**)  |

The quality work cut the rate by roughly two thirds, which is real. But it has
levelled off rather than trending to zero — of the last ten editions, **only two
were clean**, and the rest shipped one to three blanks out of seven:

```
06-08:1  06-15:0  06-22:1  06-29:2  07-06:3
07-13:2  07-20:2  07-27:0  08-03:2  08-10:1
```

Note 06-29, 07-06, 07-13, 07-20 and 08-03 all exceed `max_missing_summaries`,
which corroborates §2 as an ongoing condition rather than a one-off.

The comment in `validation.py:75-77` already records the historical 48% figure.
What is new here is the **trend and the plateau** — the fix worked and then
stopped working, at roughly one to two blanks per edition.

## 4. Correction — two claimed defects do not exist

An earlier pass in this review claimed the current edition shipped summaries
opening with a literal `# Summary` header and one model refusal
("I cannot summarize this as a news article…"). **Both claims are false.**

Scanning all 308 archived summaries:

```
md-preamble    0  (0.0%)
refusal        0  (0.0%)
trailing "..." 0  (0.0%)
```

No markdown preambles, no refusals, no trailing-ellipsis truncation anywhere in
the archive. Recorded here so the claim is not repeated: the only defect class
present is the empty/too-short summary in §2 and §3, and adding refusal
detection to `quality.py` would be solving a problem this project does not have.

## 5. Outside view — portfolio context

The one thing this repo cannot know about itself. Reviewed alongside ai-router,
burnafterreading, death-n-taxes and sprint2asi.com, and separately alongside 29
candidate business ideas.

Every one of the five projects was found to be invisible — private, undeployed,
or unpushed. Independently, the constraint that eliminated all 29 business ideas
was the absence of any public track record. **This project is the closest thing
in the portfolio to a distribution asset**: a ten-month unbroken weekly cadence,
an archive, a feed, and a brand, all finished and none of it served.

RSS is not an owned audience. Email is the only mechanism in the portfolio that
would relieve the constraint, and it needs an address to exist first.

## Actions

Ordered by what blocks what.

1. **Fix the gate (§2) before deploying.** A public archive with blank stories
   in it is worse than a private one. Establish which of the three causes it is,
   then decide whether the fix is the gate or the summariser.
2. **Point the domain at the host.** Verify the canonical URLs baked into 44
   editions and the feed all resolve.
3. **Add one privacy-preserving analytics beacon**, so readership is measurable
   from the first day rather than reconstructed later.
4. **Add email capture.** The archive is the reason to subscribe; it already
   exists.

No `docs/prompts/` directory was added — the findings above are specific enough
to act on directly, and a new convention for one task is not worth it.
